Desert textures
===============

Everything in this folder is derived from public-domain (CC0 1.0) materials
published by ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com). CC0: no rights reserved, no
attribution required; listed here so the sources can be found again.

sand-color.jpg, sand-normal.jpg
    Ground098 (wind-rippled sand), Color and NormalGL maps, 1K JPG.
    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground098
    Licence: CC0 1.0

sand-soft-normal.jpg
    Ground093C (smooth dune sand), NormalGL map, reduced to 512 px.
    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground093C
    Licence: CC0 1.0

rock-color.jpg, rock-normal.jpg
    Rock029 (red desert rock), Color map (1K) and NormalGL map (768 px).
    https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock029
    Licence: CC0 1.0

foliage-color.jpg, foliage-alpha.png
    A 2 x 2 atlas of dry grass tufts and shrubs composed from the individual
    stalks of two atlases (Color and Opacity maps, 1K JPG), re-tinted:
    Foliage002  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foliage002  Licence: CC0 1.0
    Foliage003  https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Foliage003  Licence: CC0 1.0

All files were resized and recompressed for the web.
