Forest textures
===============

All images in this folder come from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com) and are
in the public domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
They were downloaded as 1K JPG sets, reduced to the maps the forest uses and
recompressed.
